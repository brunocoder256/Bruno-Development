Yes. Mobile responsiveness is part of the build, not an extra. Layouts, typography, buttons, images and navigation are designed to adapt from small phones to large desktop screens.

Yes. I also build dashboards, portals and custom web systems where the browser becomes the workspace for your team or customers.
